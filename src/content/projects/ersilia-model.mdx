The Ersilia platform, powered by Gemini, streamlines the process of submitting new AI models. Gemini's capability to scan scientific publications allows for the automatic extraction of model-related metadata, significantly reducing the manual effort required. Users then validate this extracted information, ensuring accuracy and completeness before the models are officially submitted to the Ersilia platform. This AI-driven approach aims to accelerate the deployment and accessibility of novel AI models within the scientific community.
